Privacy

Last updated 9 November 2026. Written to be read, not to hide things.

What this site stores

  • Your Google account name, email address and profile picture, so you can sign in.
  • The interviews you finish: the settings you chose, scores, feedback and, unless you turn it off in Settings, the transcript.
  • The goal you set during onboarding, and basic usage events such as “started an interview” or “connected an AI provider” (the provider's name, never the key).
  • Your rough country and browser, taken from your request when you sign up.

What it doesn't store

  • Your AI API keys. They stay in your browser and are sent straight to the provider you chose. If you turn on the relay for a provider that blocks browser calls, the key passes through this server for that request only and is not logged or saved.
  • Your CV and job descriptions. They are read in your browser and sent only to your AI provider.

Who sees what

  • When you sign up, the site owner gets a notification with your name, email, country and browser.
  • The site owner can see your account details, how many interviews you've done and your scores. The admin panel does not show transcripts.
  • Your AI provider receives the interview conversation, your CV if you added one, and the prompts. Their own privacy policy applies to that.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with advertisers. There are no third-party trackers. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

Your controls

In Settings you can turn off transcript saving, download everything as a JSON file, or delete your account and all of its interviews permanently.

Contact

Open an issue on the project's GitHub page.

What this is

{{ app_name }} is a practice tool. Its feedback is generated by AI models and can be wrong. Treat scores as a guide for practice, not as a prediction of how a real interview will go.

Your AI provider

You connect your own AI account or key. Any cost or usage limit on that account is between you and the provider. Keep your keys private.
