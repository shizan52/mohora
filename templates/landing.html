Your answer, marked up

After each question you get your own words back with marks on them. Green is worth keeping. Pink weakened the answer. A wavy red line is a filler word.

Every answer is scored on five points for that interview type, with the two things that matter most and a rewrite of your story that keeps your facts.

Then say it again. In practice mode you can redo a single question until it lands.

Use the AI you already have

The interviewer runs on your AI account. Your key stays in your browser and goes straight to the provider; this site never stores it.

Sign in with an account

  • OpenRouter: one login for GPT, Claude, Gemini, Llama and free models
  • Puter: a free account, no key needed
  • ChatGPT Plus or Pro, once OpenAI opens sign-in to this site

Paste an API key

  • Google Gemini and Groq give free keys in about a minute
  • OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepSeek, Mistral, xAI
  • Any OpenAI-compatible server

Run it on your computer

  • Ollama or LM Studio
  • The conversation never leaves your machine; only the report you save does

Made for practice, not for feeding you answers in the real one

Plenty of tools now sell live copilots that whisper answers during a real interview. This isn't one of them. The point is to practise until you don't need one.

The code is open source, so you can read exactly what it sends to the AI and run it yourself.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Interviews run on your own AI account, so practising costs this site almost nothing, and the core practice will stay free. A free key from Google Gemini or Groq is enough for daily practice.

Do I need an API key?

No. You can sign in with an OpenRouter or Puter account instead, or run a model on your own computer with Ollama.

Can I speak my answers?

Yes. In Chrome or Edge you talk and the interviewer talks back, with live captions. You can always type instead.

Does it work in Bangla?

Yes. Choose Bangla, or Bangla mixed with English, when you set up the interview. Spoken Bangla works best in Chrome.

Where does my CV go?

It's read in your browser and sent only to the AI provider you picked, as part of the prompt. It isn't uploaded to or stored on this site.

Who can see my answers?

You. Reports are saved to your account so you can track progress. You can switch off transcript saving, export everything or delete your account in Settings. The site owner sees counts and scores, not what you said.
