Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Interviews run on your own AI account, so practising costs this site almost nothing. A free key from Google Gemini or Groq is enough for daily practice.

Do I need an API key?

{% if house.chat %}No. The built-in AI works straight away, with a daily allowance. For unlimited practice, connect your own: sign in with an OpenRouter or Puter account, paste a free Gemini or Groq key, or run a model on your computer with Ollama.{% else %}No. You can sign in with an OpenRouter or Puter account instead, or run a model on your own computer with Ollama.{% endif %}

Can I do it with my camera on?

Yes. Video call mode shows your camera next to the interviewer, like a real call. The video never leaves your device.

Does it work in Bangla?

Yes. Choose Bangla, or Bangla mixed with English, when you set up the interview. Spoken Bangla works best in Chrome and Edge.

Where does my CV go?

It's read in your browser and sent only to the AI provider you picked, as part of the prompt. It is never uploaded to this site.

Who can see my answers?

You. Reports are saved to your account so you can track progress. You can switch off transcript saving, export everything or delete your account in Settings.
